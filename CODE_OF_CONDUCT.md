# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in the INNLI project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## What we expect

- Be kind and clear. Write so that the other person can act on it.
- Talk about the work, not about the person.
- Assume a mistake is a mistake. Point it out, with how to check it.
- Accept that someone may need time to answer, and may prefer to answer in writing.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or remarks about someone's origin, gender, orientation, age, disability, neurodivergence, religion or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressure to answer faster, to call, or to decide on the spot.
- Spam and advertising that has nothing to do with the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer through the profile of the owner of this GitHub repository, or with GitHub's own "Report content" on the message. Say what happened and where. Reports are read by the maintainer only and are answered in writing.

The maintainer may remove messages, close threads, or block someone from the project, and will say why.
