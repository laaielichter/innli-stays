# INNLI visual style

The house style for all INNLI marketing visuals (posters, social posts, decks, landing pages).
Reference piece: `poster/innli-stay-access-poster.html` / `.png`. Start new visuals from that file.

## Mood
Warm, cosy B&B meets clean tech. Flat illustration with bold ink outlines, paper grain, a soft sunrise glow.
Friendly and confident, never corporate or neon-futuristic.

## Colours
| Token   | Hex       | Use |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| cream   | `#f5efe4` | Background, house walls |
| ink     | `#17202a` | Text, outlines (4–5px), buttons, chips |
| terra   | `#d9643a` | Accent: italic keyword in headline, roofs, hook line, success marks |
| sage    | `#7a9a7e` | Hills, data keys in "agent" bubbles |
| night   | `#101820` | Dark chat bubbles (the guest's AI agent) |
| glow    | `#ffcf8a` | Lit windows, sun glow, sparkles, highlights on dark |

## Type
- **Headlines:** Fraunces 700, very large (~100px on 1080 wide), tight leading (0.96) and tracking (-0.025em).
  One key word in *Fraunces italic 500, terra*.
- **Body / CTA:** Inter 500–700.
- **Hooks, labels, data:** JetBrains Mono, often uppercase with wide tracking (0.14em). Hook lines start with `//`.

## Recurring elements
- `INN`**`LI`** wordmark (LI in terra), top left; pill tag (ink outline) top right.
- Flat B&B house: terra roof, cream dotted walls, glowing windows, arched ink door, "B&B" sign.
- Two chat bubbles: dark *guest's AI agent* request ↔ white outlined *your stay via INNLI* structured answer.
- Dotted connector lines, dashed terra signal rings around the house, ✦ sparkle for AI, ✓ circle for booked.
- Sage hill with an ink band at the bottom of the scene.
- Ink pill CTA with a glow-coloured arrow; mono hashtag chips.
- Subtle noise grain overlay (multiply, ~35%).

## Copy
English, short and punchy. Core message: *your accommodation, ready for AI booking agents*.
Recurring lines: "Guests don't scroll anymore. Their agents do." · "Bookable by AI agents." ·
"Booked direct. No OTA cut." · "Is your stay agent-ready?" · "Free and open source." · #AgentReady #IndieStays #OpenSource

## Formats
Default 1080×1350 (4:5), rendered at 2× to PNG. Variants: 1080×1080 (1:1), 1080×1920 (9:16).
Keep 64–72px outer margins.
