# Videos

All videos are vertical, 720 × 1280, without sound, made with AI Edit Studio from the `*.json` story files in this folder.

| File | What | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `innli-instagram-story-720p.mp4` | Advert for owners of a B&B or small hotel | 20 s |
| `innli-booking-demo-story-720p.mp4` | A real booking on the sample stays, cut for stories | 57 s |
| `innli-camper-rental-story-720p.mp4` | Advert for camper rental: for people who rent a camper and for rental companies | 20 s |

The camper video opens with a drawn animation. Its source is `innli-camper-scene.html`: open it in a browser and call `frame(seconds)` to draw any moment. Record it at 30 frames a second to get `camper-drive.mp4`, the clip the story file refers to.

The answers in the camper video are an example in the style of the sample company *Veluwe Campers* in the demo data. They are not from a real company.
