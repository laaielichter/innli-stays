// how it works

Found, understood, booked.

Being found, being understood by an agent and actually getting booked are three different things. INNLI covers the whole route.

01 · PASSPORT

Confirm your facts once

About 40 concrete facts: enclosed garden, bed length, steps to the room, tourist tax. Each one is confirmed, unconfirmed or unknown. Only you confirm.

02 · CONNECTION

One address for every agent

Agents search your passport, check live availability and get an itemised total with every mandatory cost. Over MCP, REST, ACP or UCP.

03 · BOOKING

Booked with you, not through a middleman

The guest agrees to exact terms, the room is held, and you confirm, or it books instantly within your rules. Signed record included.

// the rule everything follows

Unknown stays unknown.

An agent that guesses sends a guest with a dog to a garden without a fence. INNLI never fills a gap.

  • Every fact has a status, a source and a check date. After a year INNLI asks you to check it again.
  • Prices are complete or say they are not. A missing mandatory cost is flagged instead of hidden.
  • Nothing is booked unless a result says booked: true. A pending request is never called a booking.
  • No hidden conversion. Prices stay in your own currency.

// for hosts

Your guests, your prices, your server.

INNLI runs on a Raspberry Pi, your own PC or a small server. It is not a chatbot and not another hotel system: it sits next to the website and booking system you already have.

Through a platform
  • 15 to 22% commission per booking
  • The platform owns the guest and the data
  • An agent that finds no live price sends the guest elsewhere
  • Your listing says what the platform lets it say
With INNLI
  • No commission on any booking
  • Guest data stays on your own installation
  • Live availability from your calendar or booking system
  • Facts you confirmed yourself, in four languages

Dashboard

Requests, room calendar, stay wishes from agents, and what agents search for in your area. In English or Dutch.

Calendars and booking systems

A live booking API, iCal from Airbnb or Booking.com, your own room calendar, or requests only. A double-booking guard watches the other channels.

WordPress plugin

Puts your confirmed facts on your site as schema.org data, verifies your domain for agents and manages AI crawler access.

Small hotels too

Room types with inventory, rate plans, 15-minute holds, staff keys and signed webhooks to your PMS.

Protected against bots

Limits on how many rooms an agent may hold, per agent and per guest. You can accept requests from identified agents only.

Camper rental

Rent out campers and motorhomes the same way: who may drive, kilometres, deposit, insurance packages and the countries the vehicle may go to. The agent checks the driver and the route before it asks, and a checklist tells the owner what must be in order first.

Stay wishes

When a guest finds nothing, the agent leaves an anonymous wish. You see the wishes your stay fits and answer with an offer.

Card guarantee

Optionally a card guarantee on your own Stripe account: authorised at booking, charged only when you confirm.

Answer assistant

Answers guest questions at once from your confirmed details and FAQ. No language model, nothing made up. The rest comes to you.

// using innli

Free to run. Open to build on.

INNLI takes no cut of a booking and sells no data. Hosts run it for free; companies can license it, or take the whole project further.

FOR HOSTS

Run it yourself, free

Everything in the repository, without limits, under the AGPL. On a Raspberry Pi, your own PC or a small server.

// quick start

Running in two commands.

Needs Node.js 22.18 or newer. A browser wizard asks for your name and your first room, then gives you your owner key. Docker and a zip download are there too.
