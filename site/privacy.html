Privacy

This page is in English only.

This website

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no forms.
  • Its fonts, images and video are served from the site itself. Nothing is loaded from another company.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes your IP address and keeps server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement. The maker of INNLI has no access to those logs.
  • Links to GitHub and npm take you to those sites, with their own rules.

The software

INNLI is software that a stay or rental company runs itself. The owner of an installation is responsible for the guest data on it. The software does not call home: the maker of INNLI receives nothing from any installation.

What an installation stores, for how long, and what an owner should arrange is in PRIVACY.md.

Who is behind this site

INNLI is made by one independent maker. Contact goes in writing, through the GitHub profile of the owner of the repository.
