# Legal documents

Where each legal matter of INNLI is written down, and what has and has not been checked.

| Subject | Document | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Licence of the code | [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) (AGPL-3.0); MIT for `agent-kit/`, GPL-2.0-or-later for `wordpress-plugin/` | Everyone who uses or changes INNLI |
| Commercial licence and prices | [COMMERCIAL.md](../../COMMERCIAL.md), [commercial-licence-agreement.md](../commercial-licence-agreement.md) | Companies that build INNLI into a closed product |
| Contributions | [CLA.md](../../CLA.md) | Anyone who sends a change |
| The name and logo | [TRADEMARK.md](../../TRADEMARK.md) | Forks, hosts, resellers |
| Other people's code in INNLI | [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), and `site/fonts/LICENSE-*.txt` for the website's fonts | Distributors |
| Personal data | [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) | Owners of an installation, and their guests |
| Security and reporting a problem | [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) | Everyone |
| Conduct in the project | [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | Contributors |

## Templates for owners

Starting points for the documents an owner needs. Fill in the parts in square brackets.

- [guest-privacy-notice.md](guest-privacy-notice.md): what a stay or rental company tells its guests about their data.
- [camper-rental-terms.md](camper-rental-terms.md): rental terms for a camper rental company. INNLI asks for a link to your terms before a rental company can be visible to agents.

## What has not been checked

- **No lawyer has reviewed these documents.** They were written with care and in plain language, with an AI assistant, by a maker who is not a lawyer.
- The templates follow what is usual in the European Union. Rules on consumer rights, insurance, tax and business registration differ per country. Have a template checked before you rely on it.
- The software's warnings about driving licences, insurance and what a rental company must have in order are rules of thumb. They are marked as such wherever they appear.
- The name INNLI is not a registered trade mark.

## What INNLI does not do

INNLI is software. It does not give legal advice, does not sell or arrange insurance, does not check a driving licence or an identity, and is not a party to the agreement between a guest and an owner.
