# Template: rental terms for a camper

A starting point for the terms of a small camper rental company. Fill in the parts in square brackets and remove what does not apply. Your insurer's conditions come first: many insurers prescribe terms for renters, and their wording must then be used. Have the result checked before you rely on it. It is not legal advice.

The numbers you enter here should be the same as in your INNLI passport, so that what an agent tells a guest matches what the guest signs.

---

## Rental terms of [name of your business]

**1. Parties.** These terms apply between [name of your business, address, registration number] ("we") and the person who rents a vehicle from us ("you").

**2. The booking.** A booking is final when we confirmed it in writing. The confirmation states the vehicle, the dates and times of pick-up and return, the price and what it includes.

**3. Who may drive.** Only the drivers named in the confirmation. Each driver is at least [23] [and at most 75] years old, has held a valid driving licence of category [B] for at least [2] years, and shows that licence and a valid identity document at pick-up. [A licence from outside the EU, EEA, Switzerland or the United Kingdom also needs an international driving permit.] We may refuse to hand over the vehicle if this is not met; the cancellation costs of article 9 then apply.

**4. Price and payment.** The price is in [euros] and [includes / excludes] VAT. It includes [the insurance of article 6, 150 km a day, cleaning]. Not included: fuel, tolls, fines, and [€ 0.30] for every kilometre above what is included.

**5. Deposit.** At pick-up you pay a deposit of [€ 1,000] [by credit card in the name of the main driver]. We return it within [14] days after the vehicle came back, less any amount you owe under these terms. We tell you in writing what we keep and why.

**6. Insurance.** The vehicle is insured for third-party liability and for damage and theft, in these countries: [list]. Your own risk is [€ 1,000] per event[, or the lower amount of the insurance package you chose]. Not covered, and for your account: damage through gross negligence, driving under the influence, driving by someone not named, driving outside the countries listed, damage to the interior through careless use, and [as your insurer states].

**7. Use.** You use the vehicle with care and as the law requires. Not allowed: smoking in the vehicle, subletting, racing or driving lessons, towing other vehicles, carrying more people than there are seats with a belt, and [pets, unless agreed]. You take the vehicle only to the countries named in the confirmation.

**8. Pick-up and return.** Together we note the state of the vehicle, the fuel level and the kilometres at pick-up and at return, in a report both of us sign. You return the vehicle on time, with [a full tank], [the toilet and waste tank emptied], and clean inside. Late return costs [€ … per hour]; leftover cleaning costs [€ …].

**9. Cancelling.** You may cancel in writing. Until [28] days before pick-up this costs nothing; after that [30]% of the price; within [7] days the full price. [The right to withdraw within 14 days that applies to many online purchases does not apply to vehicle rental for specific dates in the EU.] If we have to cancel, for instance after damage by an earlier renter, we return everything you paid and help look for another vehicle; we owe nothing more.

**10. Breakdown, damage and fines.** In case of a breakdown, accident or theft you call us at once on [phone number] and follow our instructions. You report an accident or theft to the police where the law requires it. Repairs need our permission first. Fines and tolls during the rental are yours; we pass them on with [€ …] in costs.

**11. Liability.** You are liable for damage to the vehicle during the rental up to your own risk, and fully where the insurance does not pay because of something in article 6. We are liable for damage that is our fault, up to what our insurance pays; this does not limit liability that cannot be limited by law.

**12. Your data.** We use your details to carry out this agreement, as our privacy notice says: [link].

**13. Law and disputes.** [Dutch] law applies. If you are a consumer you keep the protection of the law of the country where you live. Disputes go to the court in [place], unless the law gives you the right to choose another.
