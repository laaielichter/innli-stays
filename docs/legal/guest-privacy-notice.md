# Template: privacy notice for guests

A starting point for a stay or rental company that takes requests through INNLI. Fill in the parts in square brackets, remove what does not apply, and put the text on your website. It follows the GDPR; have it checked if you are unsure. It is not legal advice.

---

## Privacy notice of [name of your business]

**Who we are.** [Name of your business], [address], [e-mail address]. We are responsible for your personal data.

**What we collect.** When you ask to book, directly or through an AI assistant acting for you: your name, e-mail address, and if you give them your phone number and a message. [Camper rental: also the age and driving licence details of the driver, and the countries you plan to drive in.] We also keep the dates, the party, the room [or vehicle] and the price.

**Why, and on what basis.** To answer your request and carry out the booking (performing a contract, or steps before one at your request). To keep our accounts (a legal duty). We do not use your details for advertising.

**Where it is kept.** On our own booking system, [hosted by: name and country of the hosting party, or "on our own computer"]. [If you use them: our e-mail provider (name), our payment provider (name), our booking system (name).] We do not sell your data.

**How long.** Your name, contact details and message are removed automatically [180] days after your stay, or after a request that did not become a booking. What remains is the booking without your name, which we keep for our accounts for [the period the law requires in your country].

**AI assistants.** If you let an AI assistant ask or book for you, that assistant passes your details to us after you agreed. What the assistant itself keeps is decided by its provider; see their privacy notice.

**Your rights.** You may ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to remove it, or to limit its use, and you may object. Write to [e-mail address]. We answer within a month. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country [in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens].

**Changes.** Last changed on [date].
