# Commercial licence agreement

This is the agreement used for a commercial licence to INNLI, at the fixed prices in [COMMERCIAL.md](../COMMERCIAL.md). It is written to be read in five minutes. The text in square brackets is filled in per customer; nothing else changes.

> This template has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Either party may have it reviewed before signing.

---

**3. Period and price.** The licence period runs from [date] to [date, one year later]. The price is [€ 2,500 / € 9,500], excluding VAT, payable in advance. The licence starts when the payment has arrived.

**4. After the period.** The licensee may keep using the versions released on or before the last day of the period, under this agreement, without further payment. Versions released later need a new period.

**5. What is not included.** No support, maintenance, custom work, hosting or training. The licensor does not promise new versions.

**6. As it is.** The software is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. The licensor's liability under this agreement is limited to the amount the licensee paid for the current period. This does not limit liability that cannot be limited by law.

**8. The name.** This agreement gives no right to the name "INNLI" or the logo beyond saying truthfully that a product is built on INNLI. See [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md).

**9. The open version.** Nothing here limits what anyone, including the licensee, may do with the software under its open-source licences.

**10. Ending.** If the licensee does not pay, or uses the software outside clause 2, the licensor may end the agreement in writing after giving 30 days to put it right. Clause 4 then does not apply.

**11. Law.** Dutch law applies. Disputes go to the competent court in the Netherlands.

**12. In writing.** This agreement, and everything about it, is arranged in writing. E-mail counts as writing.
